# DE / FR / PL pilot research

No locale code, selector entry or translation was added. No keyword volume, CPC or live SERP data was available.

## Repository-derived facts

The strongest reusable inventory is basic math, converters, geometry, everyday tools and metric construction estimators. Finance, health and standards need market-specific review. Decimal-comma parsing/formatting now has a tested foundation but is not wired into every form.

## Model inference

- **German (recommended first):** 20-page candidate set: percentages, roots, factors, averages, equation/Pythagorean tools, area/volume/perimeter, roman numerals, numbers-to-words, date/age, cooking converter, room/wall/paint/tile/brick/concrete volume. Reuse high; terminology complexity medium-high; DIN/finance pages excluded pending native/domain review.
- **French:** same 20-page core, with broad converter/everyday potential. Reuse high; grammatical and regional terminology review required; finance must specify country/currency.
- **Polish:** same core weighted toward construction and practical calculators. Reuse high; plural/inflection complexity and Polish construction terminology require native review.

For every market, math/converters are lower risk; construction is medium/high due to practice and units; finance is high; native review is required for metadata and query terminology.

## External data needed

Validate query clusters, SERP intent/competition, country split, monetization constraints, native reviewer capacity and standards expectations. German is the first pilot recommendation because it best matches the existing technical/construction catalog, conditional on this evidence.

## Concrete research specification

For each German candidate collect country-separated DE/AT/CH query wording, volume ranges, trend/seasonality, dominant SERP intent, result features, ranking competitors, content depth, terminology/units and monetization constraints. Record source and collection date; do not turn unknowns into estimates. A native reviewer validates names, labels, examples and metadata. Construction additionally needs DIN/regional-practice review; finance and health remain outside the initial pilot.

Decision gate: compare 15–20 page low-risk German, French and Polish clusters on validated demand, competition, reviewer capacity, localization cost and revenue fit. German remains a candidate, not an approved locale.
