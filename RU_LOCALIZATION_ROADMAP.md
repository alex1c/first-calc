# Russian localization roadmap

Repository baseline: 13 Russian calculator item files and mature Russian legacy utilities. Indexability continues to require a real RU item file.

## Priority A

- Existing localized calculators: square/cube roots, exponent, logarithm, GCD, LCM, area circle/rectangle, cement, concrete volume, sand, ROI, inflation adjustment. Review terminology, examples, units, FAQs, and result copy before expanding.
- Legacy value: `chislo-propisyu`, numbers-to-words, roman numerals, percentage, roots, factors, and number-format pages. Preserve URLs and strengthen modern-equivalent links without redirects.
- First candidate cluster after native/domain review: percentage change, numbers-to-words calculator, roman-numerals converter, room/paint/brick/material calculators.

## Priority B

- Practical construction: foundations, rebar, gravel, tile, laminate, stairs, wall area. Requires Russian construction terminology and unit review; planning estimates must not imply standards compliance.
- General math/converters: averages, statistics, quadratic equation, Pythagorean theorem, volume/perimeter, cooking measurements.

## Priority C

- Finance: mortgage, loans, savings, investment, auto finance. Requires Russian-market assumptions, currency and claim review.
- Health and engineering: native/domain review is mandatory before indexing; do not translate medical advice, electrical sizing, or standards mechanically.
- Date/time and lifestyle pages after pluralization/date-format foundations mature.

No new RU calculator item was generated in this phase: quality and native review take precedence over count.
